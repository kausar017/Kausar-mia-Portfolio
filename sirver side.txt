AI Chatbot Presentation
(For ACCA & General Knowledge Support)

1. Introduction
What it is: An AI-powered virtual assistant that provides instant answers.

Purpose: To deliver accurate information on ACCA and general topics in both English & Bangla.

2. Key Features
✔ Multilingual (Supports English & Bangla)
✔ 24/7 Availability (Instant responses)
✔ Wide Knowledge Base: ACCA syllabus, exam tips, finance, science, health, and more.
✔ User-Friendly Interface (Built with Next.js + Shadcn UI)

3. How It Works
1️⃣ Input: User asks a question (text input or quick-select buttons).
2️⃣ Processing: Uses Google’s Gemini AI to analyze queries.
3️⃣ Output: Generates accurate answers in 2-3 seconds.

4. Benefits
For ACCA Students: Exam structure, fees, study tips.

For Professionals: Career guidance, financial concepts.

For Everyone: General knowledge on science, tech, health, etc.

5. Technical Stack
Frontend: Next.js, Shadcn UI

Backend: Google Gemini API

Deployment: Vercel/Netlify

6. Why Choose This Chatbot?
🚀 Fast & Reliable
🌍 Global & Local Support (ACCA + Bangla queries)
💡 Interactive Learning
